# Where the note data comes from

`pathetique-2.json` holds the notes (pitch, onset, length, staff) of Beethoven's Piano Sonata No. 8 "Pathétique", Op. 13, II. Adagio cantabile.

- Source: the Mutopia Project's typeset edition, piece 295 (https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=295), Mutopia-2011/10/25-295, from Berners 1908 (ed. A. Winterberger) and Edition Peters 1910 (Köhler & Ruthardt, IMSLP #30364). Placed in the public domain by its typesetter ("Public Domain", CC: No rights reserved). Beethoven died in 1827.
- Only facts of the composition are kept. SHA-256 of the MIDI and LilyPond files used are in the JSON (`source`).
- Checked against the printed first page and cross-checked against an independent public-domain MIDI (Wikimedia Commons, "Pathétique-2.mid", Michael Bednarek, 2009): 73 bars each, 1629 vs 1625 notes.
- Mutopia makes no claim about the accuracy of its editions.
